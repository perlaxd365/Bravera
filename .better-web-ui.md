## Design Context

### Users
Peruvian shoppers browsing a broad online marketplace, often on mobile. Brevare is expanding seasonally: Christmas products first, then summer clothing, then imported goods. The storefront should help customers scan offers, discover categories, trust prices, and reach product details quickly.

### Brand Personality
Warm, lively, and trustworthy. The experience should feel inviting and commercially clear, with seasonal energy that does not make the marketplace feel temporary or unreliable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use Brevare's yellow as the signature brand color, balanced by warm cream surfaces and deep ink typography. Use coral and evergreen sparingly for seasonal campaign accents. The supplied Falabella screenshot is a reference for merchandising density, product discovery, and campaign hierarchy; do not copy its logo or exact layout. Use real product imagery and seasonal photography with readable text overlays. Keep the storefront light, editorial, and responsive.

### Implementation Defaults
Use the existing Laravel 13, Livewire, Blade, Tailwind CSS 4, Alpine.js, and Flux stack. Preserve current routing, server-rendered product data, pricing, cart, and checkout behavior. Prefer Blade components and existing Tailwind utilities over adding a new frontend framework or UI library. Honor reduced-motion preferences and mobile touch targets.

### Design Principles
- Make the next useful action obvious: search, browse a category, or open a product.
- Show discounts only when the variant's saved compare price is greater than its actual sale price; never fabricate a reference price.
- Lead with a seasonal campaign while keeping category and product discovery close at hand.
- Design narrow screens deliberately: readable campaign copy, two-column product grids where space allows, and no hover-only information.
- Use motion to rotate campaign/product highlights with clear controls, pause behavior, and a reduced-motion alternative.
